Support

Life in the UK 2026: Made Easy · for Android and iPhone

Contact us

Email greymatterapplications@gmail.com with any question, problem or suggestion. Please say which phone you use (Android or iPhone). We usually reply within two working days.

Unlocking the full version

The full version is a one-time purchase, with no subscription. If you have bought it before, for example on a new phone or after reinstalling, open Settings → Restore purchase in the app. Use the same Google account (Android) or Apple Account (iPhone) you bought it with.

Refunds are handled by Google Play or Apple, not by us: on iPhone go to reportaproblem.apple.com; on Android use your Google Play order history.

Your progress

Your answers and progress are saved on your phone only. Settings → Reset progress clears them. Uninstalling the app also deletes them.

Daily reminder

Turn it on in Settings and allow notifications when your phone asks. If it doesn't arrive, check that notifications are allowed for the app in your phone's settings.

About the content

The app explains the official handbook, Life in the United Kingdom: A Guide for New Residents (3rd edition), in its own words. It is an independent study app, not affiliated with the UK Government or the Home Office. Official information about the test: gov.uk/life-in-the-uk-test.
