Privacy policy

Life in the UK 2026: Made Easy (Android app) · Effective 23 October 2026

Who we are

This privacy policy applies to the app "Life in the UK 2026: Made Easy" (package uk.madeeasy.lifeintheuk), published on Google Play by GreyMatterApps. For any question about privacy, email greymatterapplications@gmail.com.

Summary

The app does not collect, share or sell any personal data. There is no account or sign-in, and no advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services. We do not run any servers that the app sends data to.

Data stored on your phone only

To work, the app saves the following on your phone: your answers and scores, reading progress, the test date you enter, your reminder time, memory hooks you write, your settings, and whether you have unlocked the full version. This information stays on your phone and is never sent to us or to anyone else by the app.

If Android backup is switched on for your Google account, Android may include this saved information in your device backup so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup is managed by Google under Google's privacy policy; we cannot see it.

Purchases

The optional one-time unlock is sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment; we never receive your card or payment details. The app asks Google Play, on your phone, whether the unlock has been bought, so it can open the full version. Google's privacy policy applies to Google Play: policies.google.com/privacy.

Permissions

  • Internet: used only to talk to Google Play about the purchase. All study content is inside the app and works offline.
  • Notifications: only if you turn on the daily reminder. The reminder is scheduled on your phone; nothing is sent to us.

Security

Your saved information is kept in the app's private storage on your phone, protected by Android's app sandbox. The app does not transmit it anywhere.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your information stays on your phone until you delete it. You can delete it at any time with Settings → Reset progress in the app, by clearing the app's storage in your phone's settings, or by uninstalling the app. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing for us to delete.

Children

The app is intended for adults preparing for the Life in the UK test. It is not designed for children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.

Disclaimer

Life in the UK 2026: Made Easy is an independent study app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or representing the UK Government, the Home Office or any government body. Official information: gov.uk/life-in-the-uk-test.
